# Sources log - Abercrombie & Fitch Co. (ANF)

This log adds three primary sources beyond the Fiscal 2025 Form 10-K already used in the report. Each entry records the dated, attributable source and the specific sentence or data point to cite.

## 1. Fiscal 2023 Form 10-K (prior filing)

- **Source:** [Abercrombie & Fitch Co., Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended February 3, 2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884024000019/anf-20240203.htm), filed April 1, 2024; Item 1, "Strategy and Key Business Priorities," p. 6; Item 7, "Current Trends and Outlook," p. 32.
- **Line taken:** Management's Fiscal 2024 objective was to drive sales growth across regions and brands, primarily through marketing and store investment.
- **Promise check:** Fiscal 2024 total net sales rose 16% to $4.95 billion; Abercrombie sales grew 16% and Hollister sales grew 15%. **Outcome source:** [Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended February 1, 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884025000013/anf-20250201.htm), filed March 31, 2025; Item 7, "Results of Operations," p. 34.

## 2. Fiscal 2024 Form 10-K (prior filing)

- **Source:** [Abercrombie & Fitch Co., Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended February 1, 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884025000013/anf-20250201.htm), filed March 31, 2025; Item 7, "Current Trends and Outlook," pp. 30-32.
- **Line taken:** Management's Fiscal 2025 priority was to drive sales growth across regions and brand families through marketing, store investment, and selected partnership channels.
- **Promise check:** Fiscal 2025 total net sales increased 6%, but Abercrombie sales declined 1% while Hollister sales grew 15%; therefore, company-wide sales grew but the stated broad brand-family growth did not occur. **Outcome source:** the [Fiscal 2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018840/000101884026000012/anf-20260131.htm) already used in the report, Item 7, "Results of Operations," p. 35.

## 3. Company earnings release / CEO statement

- **Source:** [Abercrombie & Fitch Co. Reports Fourth Quarter and Full Year Results](https://abercrombieandfitchcompany.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/abercrombie-fitch-co-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-7/), Abercrombie & Fitch Co., March 4, 2026; CEO Fran Horowitz statement.
- **Line taken:** Horowitz said the company's Fiscal 2026 goals were to grow net sales, deliver another year of double-digit operating margin, and grow earnings per share while making strategic investments.
- **Why it matters:** This is management's dated forward-looking commitment after Fiscal 2025; it can be tested against later quarterly releases and the next Form 10-K.

## Citation-ready comparison

The Fiscal 2023 filing's broad-based growth objective was met in Fiscal 2024, when both brands grew by double digits. The Fiscal 2024 filing again targeted growth across brand families, but Fiscal 2025 growth was uneven: Hollister grew while Abercrombie declined. The Fiscal 2025 report's WATCH-DEFER conclusion remains appropriate because its concern is not whether the earlier growth occurred, but whether balanced growth and margin stability will persist. **Source for the last sentence:** UNSOURCED; author judgment.
