## Investment decision

- **Company:** Abercrombie & Fitch Co. (NYSE: ANF)
- **Valuation date:** September 1, 2026
- **Decision user:** Investment Committee
- **Initial thesis:** WATCH-DEFER

**Decision statement.**  
Defer initiating a position while monitoring ANF. Fiscal 2025 revenue growth and net cash support a constructive operating base, but the sales decline at the Abercrombie brand and margin pressure make the durability of earnings uncertain. Reassess after evidence that brand demand is broadening and that gross-margin pressure from tariffs and product/channel mix is stabilizing.

## Initial investment thesis

### What is known

- Fiscal 2025 net sales increased 6% to $5.266 billion; comparable sales increased 3%. (10-K, MD&A - Results of Operations, p. 35.)
- Growth was uneven by brand: Hollister net sales rose 15% with 13% comparable-sales growth, while Abercrombie net sales declined 1% and comparable sales declined 7%. (10-K, MD&A - Results of Operations, p. 35.)
- GAAP operating margin fell to 13.3% from 15.0%; management identified approximately $90 million of adverse net tariff impact in cost of sales. (10-K, MD&A - Results of Operations, pp. 35-36.)
- The company reported $759.540 million of cash and equivalents and no interest-bearing borrowings outstanding at January 31, 2026 under the stated bridge definition. (10-K, Note 2, p. 52; Note 13, p. 69.)

### Consequential assumptions

- Hollister can sustain positive comparable-sales growth without relying on unusually favorable promotions or temporary fashion demand. This matters because it was the principal source of brand-level growth in Fiscal 2025.
- Gross margin can stabilize despite tariffs, product mix, and channel mix. This matters because margin pressure can offset revenue growth and reduce cash generation.
- The Abercrombie brand can return to at least stable comparable sales. This matters because a persistent decline would make company-level growth more concentrated in Hollister.

### Unknowns and open questions

- The valuation implied by ANF's market price at the valuation date and whether it already discounts a normalization of sales growth and margins.
- The drivers of the Abercrombie comparable-sales decline and management's evidence that the decline is temporary rather than structural.
- The duration and recoverability of tariff, product-mix, and channel-mix pressure on gross margin.
