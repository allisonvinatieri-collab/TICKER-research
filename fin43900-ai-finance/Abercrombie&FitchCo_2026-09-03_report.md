# Abercrombie & Fitch Co. (NYSE: ANF) - Company Research Report

**Call: WATCH-DEFER.** The company has continued to grow total sales and retains a net-cash balance sheet, but Fiscal 2025 growth was concentrated in Hollister while Abercrombie declined and operating margin fell. **Sources:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 7, pp. 35-36; Item 8, Note 2, p. 52; Item 8, Note 13, p. 69.

## Business and latest results

Abercrombie & Fitch Co. is a global, digitally led omnichannel retailer operating the Abercrombie and Hollister brand families. **Source:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 1, Business, pp. 5-6.

Fiscal 2025 net sales increased 6% to $5.266 billion, and comparable sales increased 3%. **Source:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 7, Results of Operations, p. 35.

Hollister net sales increased 15% to $2.743 billion and comparable sales increased 13%. **Source:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 7, Results of Operations, Net Sales by Brand, p. 35.

Abercrombie net sales declined 1% to $2.524 billion and comparable sales declined 7%. **Source:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 7, Results of Operations, Net Sales by Brand, p. 35.

GAAP operating margin declined to 13.3% from 15.0%; management attributed approximately $90 million of adverse net tariff impact to cost of sales and also cited unfavorable product and channel mix. **Source:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 7, Results of Operations, pp. 35-36.

## Balance sheet and share base

Cash and cash equivalents were $759.540 million at January 31, 2026. **Source:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 8, Note 2 - Cash and Equivalents, p. 52.

The company had no borrowings outstanding under its ABL Facility at January 31, 2026, and its senior secured notes had been fully redeemed on July 15, 2024. **Source:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 7, Credit Facility, p. 39; Item 8, Note 13 - Borrowings, p. 69.

Fiscal 2025 diluted weighted-average shares were 48.476 million, diluted EPS was $10.46, and 1.157 million incremental shares came from dilutive share-based compensation awards. **Source:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Operations, p. 46; Item 8, Note 2 - Net Income per Share, p. 59.

## Execution history: what management said and what happened

In its Fiscal 2023 Form 10-K, management identified Fiscal 2024 sales growth across regions and brands, supported by marketing and store investment, as an objective. **Source:** Form 10-K filed April 1, 2024, Item 1, Strategy and Key Business Priorities, p. 6; Item 7, Current Trends and Outlook, p. 32.

Fiscal 2024 delivered total net sales growth of 16%; Abercrombie sales grew 16% and Hollister sales grew 15%. **Source:** Form 10-K filed March 31, 2025, Item 7, Results of Operations, p. 34.

In its Fiscal 2024 Form 10-K, management again prioritized sales growth across regions and brand families through marketing, store investment, and selected partnership channels. **Source:** Form 10-K filed March 31, 2025, Item 7, Current Trends and Outlook, pp. 30-32.

Fiscal 2025 total sales grew 6%, but the outcome was not balanced across the two brand families because Hollister grew while Abercrombie declined. **Source:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 7, Results of Operations, p. 35.

On March 4, 2026, CEO Fran Horowitz said management's Fiscal 2026 goals were to grow net sales, deliver another year of double-digit operating margin, and grow earnings per share while investing for long-term growth. **Source:** Abercrombie & Fitch Co., "Reports Fourth Quarter and Full Year Results," March 4, 2026, CEO statement.

## Investment view

The Fiscal 2024 results show that management previously achieved broad-based brand growth, but the Fiscal 2025 mix of a declining Abercrombie brand and lower operating margin makes the durability of earnings less clear. **Sources:** Form 10-K filed March 31, 2025, Item 7, p. 34; Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 7, pp. 35-36; author judgment.

The key evidence to monitor is whether Abercrombie returns to stable comparable sales, whether Hollister retains positive comparable-sales growth, and whether tariff and product/channel-mix pressure stabilizes. **Sources:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 7, pp. 35-36; author judgment.

## Conclusion

**WATCH-DEFER.** I would wait for evidence that growth is again balanced across the brands and that gross-margin pressure is stabilizing before initiating a position. **Sources:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 7, pp. 35-36; author judgment.

This call would change if subsequent results show sustained positive comparable sales at both brand families alongside stabilized gross margin. **Sources:** Form 10-K filed March 26, 2026, Item 7, pp. 35-36; author judgment.
