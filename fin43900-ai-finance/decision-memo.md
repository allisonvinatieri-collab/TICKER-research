# Investment Committee Memo — Abercrombie & Fitch Co. (ANF)

## Decision

**Watch/defer; do not initiate today.** The September 9, 2026 market price of **$152.08** is below the base FCFF value of **$183.18 per diluted share**, but the valuation depends on revenue growth broadening beyond Hollister and on partial margin recovery. Those operating conditions are not yet sufficiently evidenced.

## Valuation range and method judgment

The reconciled FCFF DCF range is **$151.79–$233.24/share**, spanning 10% WACC/2% terminal growth through 8% WACC/3% terminal growth. The base case uses 9% WACC and 2.5% terminal growth and produces $183.18/share.

This is a range, not a target price. Terminal value is 75.1% of enterprise value, which makes the conclusion sensitive to durable long-run cash generation. The market-implied reverse DCF requires a 6.62-percentage-point uniform reduction to the base revenue-growth path, holding margin, WACC, terminal growth, and bridge inputs constant.

The peer-method cross-check uses AEO and URBN under the predeclared policy. At the September 9 price, ANF trades at **8.64x FY2025 GAAP EV/EBIT** and **14.54x FY2025 GAAP P/E**, versus the two-peer mean of **11.36x** and **15.41x**. This supports the view that the price does not require the base DCF operating case, but it does not resolve the company-specific risk that ANF's growth is concentrated in Hollister. The DCF and peer outputs are not mechanically averaged.

## Evidence and assumptions

Fiscal 2025 sales increased 6% to $5.266bn, but performance was uneven: Hollister sales grew 15% while Abercrombie sales declined 1%. GAAP operating margin fell to 13.3% from 15.0%, with management citing about $90m of adverse tariff impact plus unfavorable mix. See the FY2025 Form 10-K in `sources.md`.

The base forecast fades revenue growth from 5% to 3% and assumes gross margin improves from 61.0% to 62.0%. The bridge uses $284.576m of valuation-date excess cash, $0 interest-bearing debt, and 44.864m diluted shares. Lease liabilities are treated as operating consistently with the forecast's operating-expense treatment.

## Risks and monitoring triggers

- **Initiation condition:** evidence that Abercrombie comparable sales stabilize or turn positive, Hollister remains positive, and margin pressure stabilizes—combined with a peer cross-check that does not contradict the FCFF range.
- **Downside condition:** Hollister growth slows materially while Abercrombie remains negative, or tariffs/product and channel mix prevent the assumed margin recovery.
- **Model condition:** reassess if the share basis, excess-cash policy, lease treatment, or valuation date changes.

## Committee ask

Approve a **watch/defer** status. Revisit after the next reported brand-level sales and margin evidence; do not initiate solely because the DCF and peer cross-check indicate apparent value.
